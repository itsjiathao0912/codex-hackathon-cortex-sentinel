Build Directions

Autonomous &

02
AI-Native Products
& Operations
Products, services, and operations fundamentally redesigned with AI - or something that simply couldn't exist before

Judging Criteria
01
Problem & Solution Fit
Is the problem real?
Is the solution the right answer to it?
02
Build Quality
Does it work and hold up under use?
We judge the product - not the pitch.
03
Insight & Originality
Is the approach genuinely novel, or just common industry practice?
04
Real-World Value
Based on the working demo - who benefits, and how significantly?
05
Alignment with Build Direction
Does the build meaningfully reflect at least one build direction?
06
Use of Codex
How deeply was Codex leveraged to build this product?
